# **Municipal Civic Issue Management & Grievance Tracking System**

### **1\. Problem Statement**

In many local areas, when citizens face civic issues such as **potholes, damaged roads, broken streetlights, garbage accumulation, drainage problems, or water-related issues**, reporting them often involves approaching local representatives or officials first.

The complaint may then pass through multiple people before reaching the appropriate municipal department. This can lead to **delays, lack of transparency, difficulty tracking complaints, and uncertainty about who is responsible for resolving the issue**.

### **2\. Proposed Solution**

Build a **centralized digital platform** that allows citizens to directly report civic issues to the appropriate authority and track their resolution.

A citizen can submit an issue with details such as:

- Issue category
- Description
- Location
- Photograph/video
- Date of reporting

The system then routes the complaint to the **relevant department/authority**, where an authorized officer can review, assign, update, and resolve it.

### **3\. Basic Workflow**

**Citizen reports issue**  
↓  
**System categorizes/routes complaint**  
↓  
**Relevant department receives it**  
↓  
**Officer is assigned**  
↓  
**Issue is investigated/resolved**  
↓  
**Citizen receives status/update**

### **4\. Key Features**

- Citizen registration/login
- Civic issue reporting
- Location and image attachment
- Complaint categorization
- Department/authority routing
- Complaint status tracking
- Officer/admin dashboard
- Assignment and resolution tracking
- Notifications/status updates
- Escalation for long-pending complaints
- Basic analytics on complaints and resolution time

### **5\. Future Scope**

The system could later be expanded with:

- Duplicate complaint detection
- Location-based issue clustering
- Interactive municipal dashboards/maps
- AI-based image classification of civic issues
- Prediction of frequently occurring problem areas
- Automated prioritization of critical complaints

### **6\. Technologies/Concepts**

The project can directly utilize our current **React, JavaScript, Node.js, Express, HTTP, middleware, Prisma, and SQL/database** concepts, while leaving room for additional technologies and AI features as we learn them.
