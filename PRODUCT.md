# Fix My City

Audience: citizens and municipal staff in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Task: report a civic issue, understand who owns it and follow it through confirmed resolution. Staff need a clear assignment queue and evidence-backed updates.

Source of truth: supplied PS1.md functional brief, syllabus screenshot and user's final explicit scope. Local stack: HTML/CSS/vanilla JavaScript, Node.js, Express MVC, Prisma and SQLite only. No frontend frameworks, interactive map or heatmap.

User-selected city: Mumbai. Assumed visual world: forest-green civic service desk with warm white surfaces; readable and responsive. Operate mode. Photographs, optional MP4/WebM video and text addresses, optional manually entered coordinates; duplicate detection is heuristic. All sample content is fictional. This is an independent local demo without government integration.

Staff land in their operational workspace; citizen community and reporting actions are absent from officer/admin navigation and direct routes redirect. Intake is Mumbai-only; historical non-sample locations are preserved.
