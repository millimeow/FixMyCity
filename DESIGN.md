# Interface system

Operate surface: community reporting and role workspaces. Forest green #185346 actions, warm white #f7f8f4 canvas, charcoal #202f2b text, muted green #64716b support text. Readable native sans typography; 32px desktop headings / 27px mobile; functional text at least 11px. Strong focus rings, reduced-motion support, full form labels and escaped dynamic copy.

Sidebar at desktop; dismissible navigation on mobile. Community board uses filterable category cards, with list option on desktop. Report forms and details occupy dedicated pages. Timeline, evidence and assignment facts stay together. Admin and officer queues reuse the board; server-enforced permissions control available actions. Category graphics are geometric symbols, explicitly marked as sample content rather than photographs.
