# FixMyCity 🏙️
### Civic Issue Reporting and Resolution System

FixMyCity is a web-based platform that allows citizens to report civic issues such as potholes, damaged roads, garbage accumulation, broken streetlights, drainage problems, and water supply issues. It helps municipal authorities manage complaints, assign them to the relevant departments, and track their resolution efficiently.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Citizens often face difficulties reporting local civic issues and tracking their progress. Traditional complaint processes can lead to delays, poor communication, and a lack of transparency.

FixMyCity aims to simplify this process by providing a centralized digital platform for reporting, managing, and resolving civic complaints.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Make civic issue reporting simple and accessible.
- Connect citizens directly with municipal authorities.
- Improve complaint tracking and resolution.
- Increase transparency and accountability.
- Reduce delays in addressing civic problems.

## ✨ Key Features

- **User Authentication:** Register and log in securely.
- **Report Civic Issues:** Submit complaints with descriptions, categories, and locations.
- **Image Uploads:** Attach photographs of reported problems.
- **Complaint Tracking:** Track complaint status and progress.
- **Department Assignment:** Route complaints to the appropriate municipal department.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Manage complaints and assign responsible officers.
- **Status Updates:** Keep citizens informed about complaint progress.
- **Escalation System:** Highlight complaints that remain unresolved for too long.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** View complaint statistics and resolution progress.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. A citizen registers or logs in to the platform.
2. The citizen submits a complaint with issue details and location.
3. The system categorizes the complaint and routes it to the relevant department.
4. An authorized officer reviews and manages the complaint.
5. The complaint status is updated as work progresses.
6. The citizen tracks the complaint until the issue is resolved.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

The project is planned using the following technologies:

- **Frontend:** React.js, JavaScript, HTML, CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** SQL database with Prisma ORM
- **API:** RESTful APIs and HTTP
- **Tools:** Git, GitHub, npm

*The technology stack may evolve as development progresses.*
